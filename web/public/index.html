INSIDE THE GAME

The world comes first.

A small chat window. Names above characters. Login and choose an adventure first. Progress follows your account; menus stay out of the world.

Screenshots are from the current desktop client and a two-client development server session. Select an image to view it at full size.

Your server adventure

New adventures begin at the family farm, skipping the boat intro. Your local ROM supplies the campaign. Your account holds the adventures and saves on the server. Continue on another device after signing in.

A shared adventure

Sign in from the client, chat with other players, and see nearby adventurers with names above their characters. Keep chat open while you explore.

Desktop + Android

Keyboard and gamepad on desktop. Landscape play, multitouch controls, and an optional analog stick, adjustable controls, and full-screen stretch on Android.

Menu → Optional ROMs accepts your local Iru (GBC) and Caravan Heart (GBA) sources. Their shared-world regions are still in development; importing a ROM does not unlock them yet.

Early alpha · still taking shapeShared-world coverage is limited. Original online battles, trades, and breeding are in development and have not been verified end to end.
ANDROID / PLAY YOUR WAY

Room for your thumbs.

Larger movement and action buttons. Choose a D-pad or analog stick, change control size, and stretch the game to fill your screen in Menu → Settings.

DOWNLOAD / 0.2.4 ALPHA

Choose your client.

Download the client and follow the prompt to select your local USA Cobi’s Journey ROM. Create an account or sign in directly in the game, then select your adventure. An internet connection is required. Your ROM stays on your device. No ROM is included or uploaded.

Unofficial fan project; not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix or Nintendo. No ROM is included or provided. You must have the legal right to use your own compatible ROM. Read the project notice.
