Meet other players

Desktop + Android

Play with a keyboard or gamepad on desktop. On Android, choose touch controls, adjust their size, and fill the screen.

Optional Iru and Caravan Heart ROMs can be imported from Menu → Optional ROMs. Their regions are not available to play yet.

Alpha availabilityNearby players and chat are available. Player battles, trading, breeding, parties, and additional regions are not ready to play yet.
ANDROID

Play on Android.

Choose a D-pad or analog stick, adjust the controls, and stretch the display to fill your screen in Menu → Settings.

DOWNLOAD / 0.2.5 ALPHA

Choose your client.

Download the client and follow the prompt to select your local USA Cobi’s Journey ROM. Create an account or sign in directly in the game, then select your adventure. An internet connection is required. Your ROM stays on your device. No ROM is included or uploaded.
